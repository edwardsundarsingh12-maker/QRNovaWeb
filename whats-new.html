Version 1.0.0 New October 2026
  • QR Nova is here. Create, design and scan QR codes and barcodes, with no account needed.
  • 31 QR content types across Essentials, Social, Payments, Business & Events and Links & Stores, each with a live preview.
  • Design studio with 15 body shapes, 5 eye frames, 5 eye dots, gradients, 7 background modes, logos from photos, 133 icons, text or emoji, plus undo and redo.
  • Scannability check and Auto-fix that warns about low contrast or an oversized logo and repairs the style in one tap.
  • 18 templates, Surprise me, and saved style presets you can share as a style code.
  • Barcode generator for Code 128, EAN-13, UPC-A, EAN-8, Code 39 and ITF.
  • Scanner for 12 formats with torch, zoom, scan from gallery and smart one-tap actions.
  • Export to PNG, JPG, WEBP, SVG and PDF up to 4K, with transparent backgrounds and printing.
  • Downloads, folders, history and global search, batch generation, backup & restore, and four home screen widgets.
