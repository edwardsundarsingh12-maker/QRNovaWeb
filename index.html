Features

Everything a QR code can be

Create it, style it, check it still scans, and export it at print quality, all in one app that works without an account.

A code for whatever you share

31 content types: websites, WiFi, contacts, events, locations, WhatsApp, Instagram and UPI or PayPal payments. Each one has its own simple form and a live preview.

A studio, not a settings page

15 body shapes, 5 eye frames and 5 eye dots, solid colours or linear, radial and sweep gradients, and 7 background modes including your own photo. Undo and redo every change.

Your logo in the middle

Drop in a photo, pick from 133 built-in icons, or use text and emoji. Adjust size, padding, shape, border, shadow and rotation until it looks just right.

It checks the code still scans

Low contrast, a faded colour, an oversized logo or a busy background gets flagged as you design. One tap on Auto-fix repairs the style so the code reads again.

18 templates and your own presets

Start from Royal Purple, Candy Pop, Midnight, Sunset, Forest Leaf or Gold Leaf. Save designs you love as presets and share them with a friend as a style code.

Scans 12 formats in a flash

QR, Data Matrix, Aztec, PDF417 and every common product barcode. Torch, zoom, scan from a gallery image, and a smart result card that joins WiFi or opens a link in one tap.

Barcodes for products and stock

Code 128, EAN-13, UPC-A, EAN-8, Code 39 and ITF, with input checked for each format so the barcode you print is one a till can read.

Export ready for print

PNG, JPG, WEBP, vector SVG or PDF, from 512 px up to 4K or a custom size, with transparent backgrounds for design work. Print straight from the app.

How it works

From idea to printed code in a minute

Four steps, and the preview updates the whole way through.

Choose what it does

Pick a type from the Generate hub, or search for it, and fill in the details. Your QR code appears as you type.

Make it yours

Apply a template or tap Surprise me, then fine-tune shapes, colours, background and logo in the studio.

Let it check itself

QR Nova warns you if the style makes the code hard to read, and Auto-fix sorts it out in one tap.

Save, share or print

Export in the format and size you need. It lands in Downloads, fully editable, whenever you want to change it.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is QR Nova free?

Yes. Every QR type, the full design studio, the scanner, barcodes, batch mode, widgets and exports up to 1024 px are free, with ads. Premium removes ads and unlocks HD and 4K exports, all Pro templates and unlimited saved presets. Free users can also unlock a single HD export, Pro template or extra preset by watching a short ad.

How much is Premium?

Choose monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase, all through Google Play. Prices are shown in your local currency in the app, and you can restore a purchase on a new phone with the same Google account.

Will my styled QR code still scan?

QR Nova checks this for you while you design. If the contrast is too low, a colour is too faint, the logo is too large or the background is too busy, you'll see a warning, and Auto-fix adjusts the style so the code reads reliably. For heavy styling, setting error correction to H (30%) gives extra headroom.

Can I change where a printed QR code points?

No. QR Nova creates static codes: the content is stored inside the code itself, so it works forever without any server, but it can't be redirected after printing. If you may need to change it, point the code at a page you control.

Do I need to sign up or stay online?

No account is needed, and creating, styling, scanning and exporting all work offline. Your codes, scans and presets are stored only on your phone. An internet connection is used just to show ads and handle purchases.

Which formats can I export?

PNG, JPG, WEBP, vector SVG and PDF, in 512, 1024, 2048 (HD) or 4096 px (4K), or any custom size from 128 to 8192 px. PNG, WEBP and SVG support transparent backgrounds.
